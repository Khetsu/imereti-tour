            Frequently asked questions

            How do I book a seat?

            Fill in the form on the home page and send it via WhatsApp, or call our numbers.

            How much do I pay when booking?

            At least 25%. You can also pay the full amount.

            Which days do buses run?

            From Georgia on Wednesdays and Saturdays, from Moscow on Tuesdays and Saturdays.

            Do you carry parcels?

            Yes, in both directions. Call or message us for details.

            Where can I board?

            Batumi, Kobuleti, Poti, Kutaisi, Tbilisi. In Moscow at metro Annino.

            Can I cancel my booking?

            Yes. If you cancel, we refund the amount you paid. Message us on WhatsApp.
